## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW (Ta’aruf & Maqsad)
Yeh project Saudi Arabia mein ride-hailing drivers (Jeeny, Uber, Bolt) ke liye aik advance auto-accept bot application aur license management system tayyar karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Is solution ka official brand name **"Driving Care"** hoga. 

Is system ke zariye drivers apni man-pasand rides auto-accept kar sakenge aur Bilal bhai drivers ko monthly 30-day keys sell karke subscription business chala sakenge.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK & KEY FEATURES (Kaam Ki Tafseel)

### A. Driver Mobile App ("Driving Care" Android APK)
1. **Custom Branding:**
   - App ka official naam: **Driving Care**
   - New custom logo aur splash screen
   - Bilal bhai ka official Saudi WhatsApp number integration (Driver 1-click se Bilal bhai se WhatsApp par contact karega)
2. **Smart Ride Acceptance Engine:**
   - **Target Price Filter:** Min Price (e.g., 10 SAR) se Max Price (e.g., 100 SAR) tak auto-accept.
   - **Auto-Reject:** Low-fare rides (e.g., 10 SAR se kam) ko automatically reject karne ka option.
   - **Pickup ETA Filter:** Driver khud select karega ke kitne time ke andar wali ride accept karni hai (e.g., 5–10 minutes).
   - **Area & Location Blocklist:** Airport (مطار), Al Batha (البطحاء), Shamisi, waghera ko blacklist karne ka feature taake unwanted areas ki ride accept na ho.
   - **Payment Modes:** Cash aur Card rides ke separate toggles.
3. **Driver Accessibility & Floating HUD:**
   - Screen par 1-tap ON/OFF floating button (Over-the-screen overlay).
   - Jeeny / Uber / Bolt ke sath seamless real-time operation.

### B. 30-Day License Management System (Admin Web Portal)
1. **Monthly Key Generation:** Bilal bhai ke liye web portal jahan se woh 30 din ki valid keys generate kar sakenge.
2. **Auto Expiry:** Har key theek 30 din baad automatically expire ho jayegi. Expiry ke baad driver ka bot chalna band ho jayega jab tak woh monthly fee dekar renew na karwaye.
3. **Driver Control:** Kisi bhi driver ki key ko activate, suspend ya delete karne ka mukammal ikhtiyar.
